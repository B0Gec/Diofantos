### **Recursive Definition**

Let $ a(n) $ denote the $ n $-th term of the sequence. The recursive formula for $ a(n) $ is as follows:

- **Base case**:  
  $$
  a(0) = 1
  $$

- **Recursive case**:  
  For $ n \geq 1 $, let $ k = n + 1 $.  
  - If $ k $ is **prime**, then:  
    $$
    a(n) = k
    $$
  - If $ k $ is **composite**, let $ p $ be the **smallest prime factor** of $ k $. Then:  
    $$
    a(n) = \max(p, a\left(\frac{k}{p} - 1\right))
    $$

---

### **Explanation**

- The base case $ a(0) = 1 $ corresponds to $ n+1 = 1 $, whose largest prime factor is 1.
- For $ n \geq 1 $, we evaluate $ k = n+1 $.
  - If $ k $ is prime, its largest prime factor is itself.
  - If $ k $ is composite, we factor it into its smallest prime factor $ p $ and the corresponding quotient $ \frac{k}{p} $. The largest prime factor of $ k $ is then the maximum of $ p $ and the largest prime factor of $ \frac{k}{p} $, which is $ a\left(\frac{k}{p} - 1\right) $.

- Therefore, $ a(10) = 11 $.
