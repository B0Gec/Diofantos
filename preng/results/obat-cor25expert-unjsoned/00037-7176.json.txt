### **Conclusion:**

- For **odd $ n $**, the term is the **sum of the divisors** of $ n + 1 $.
- For **even $ n $**, the pattern is **not clearly defined** based on the given data. The values do not consistently match $ \sigma(m) $, $ \tau(m) $, or their combinations.

$$

$$

For even $ n $, further information or a different approach is needed to determine the pattern.
