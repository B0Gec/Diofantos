a_0 &= 0 \\

- $ a_2 = 1 $: Only the string "00" contains two consecutive zeros.

### **Explanation of the Recurrence**

This recurrence is derived from the complementary problem: counting the number of bit strings of length $ n $ that **do not** contain two consecutive zeros. Let $ b_n $ be the number of such strings. Then:

$$
b_n = b_{n-1} + b_{n-2}
$$

This is the Fibonacci recurrence, with base cases $ b_0 = 1 $, $ b_1 = 2 $, and so on.

Since the total number of bit strings of length $ n $ is $ 2^n $, the number of strings with at least one occurrence of "00" is:

$$
a_n = 2^n - b_n
$$

By substituting $ b_n = 2^n - a_n $ into the Fibonacci recurrence and simplifying, we derive the recurrence for $ a_n $ as:

$$
a_n = 3a_{n-1} - a_{n-2} - 2a_{n-3}
$$
