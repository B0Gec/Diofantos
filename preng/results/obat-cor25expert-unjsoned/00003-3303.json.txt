- $ a_n $: the number of valid ternary strings of length $ n $ that do **not** contain "00".

- $ b_n $: number of valid strings of length $ n $ that **end in 0**.
- $ c_n $: number of valid strings of length $ n $ that **end in 1 or 2**.

$$
a_n = b_n + c_n
$$

  $$
  b_n = c_{n-1}
  $$

  $$
  c_n = 2a_{n-1}
  $$

$$
a_n = b_n + c_n = c_{n-1} + 2a_{n-1}
