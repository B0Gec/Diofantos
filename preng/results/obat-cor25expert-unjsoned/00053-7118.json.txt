The Euler numbers are a sequence of integers that appear in the Taylor series expansions of the secant and tangent functions. Specifically, the even-indexed Euler numbers are the coefficients in the expansion of the secant function:

$$
\sec(x) = \sum_{n=0}^{\infty} \frac{E_{2n}}{(2n)!} x^{2n}

### **Recurrence Relation**

The Euler numbers satisfy the following recurrence relation:

$$
E(n) = -\sum_{k=0}^{n-1} \binom{n-1}{k} E(k)
$$

for $ n \geq 1 $, with the initial condition $ E(0) = 1 $.

However, this recurrence applies to the full sequence of Euler numbers (both even and odd indices). For the **even-indexed Euler numbers** (i.e., the secant numbers), the recurrence is more complex and typically involves generating functions or combinatorial interpretations.
